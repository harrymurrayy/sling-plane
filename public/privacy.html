Slingwing Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

In short: Slingwing does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the game stores

Your progress (coins, upgrades, levels, stars, best distances and settings) is saved only on your own device. It never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not ask for or collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or usage data, and it does not use any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services.

Internet access

The game works offline. It does not send data to any server.

Children

Because the game collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if online leaderboards are added, this page will be updated before the change ships and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised at github.com/harrymurrayy/sling-plane/issues.
